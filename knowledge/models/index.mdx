---
title: Models
description: Amp and pedal model targets, separated from reusable circuit-cell knowledge.
---

Model pages describe musical targets: a named amp family, pedal family, or rig-facing device. They may reference physical parts and circuit cells, but they should not become reusable circuit-cell documentation.

Use:

- `models/amps/` for amp families and variants.
- `models/pedals/` for pedal families grouped by category.
- `circuits/` for reusable circuit cells, passive networks, validation rules, and shared solver behavior.

This separation keeps circuit-cell engineering reusable while letting each model page explain the musical target, topology choice, controls, validation state, and variant assumptions.
