---
title: Component Vocabulary
description: Canonical naming for physical parts, circuit cells, stages, devices, and rigs.
---

Greybound uses circuit knowledge, but it is not currently a full component-exact
SPICE runtime. Keep these levels distinct so code, docs, lab reports, and UI
descriptors do not overclaim what a model contains.

## Canonical Levels

| Level | Meaning | Examples | Preferred name |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Physical part | One real electronic part or primitive model. | `100k` plate resistor, `22nF` coupling capacitor, ECC83 triode section, diode, pot, transformer winding. | `part`, `physical_part`, or the exact part kind |
| Circuit cell | A reusable small subcircuit with explicit voltage/current meaning. | common-cathode stage, diode clipper, cathode follower, passive tone stack, sag cell. | `cell` or `CircuitCell` |
| Stage | A model-level block in an amp or pedal signal path. It may contain many parts or cells. | `first_stage`, `tone_stack`, `phase_inverter`, `power_stage`, `supply_network`. | `stage` |
| Device | A complete amp, pedal, cab, or effect model. | Nox30, Minotaur, Springfield, None Star. | `device`, `amp`, `pedal`, `effect` |
| Rig | Musical topology and controls. | Minotaur -> Nox30 -> Springfield -> cab IR. | `rig` |

## Rule

Use `component` only when the text really means a physical part, or when keeping
a backwards-compatible public API that already used the old word.

For model-boundary data, use `stage`:

- `StageBoundary`: a named observable boundary such as `first_stage` or
  `tone_stack`.
- `StageBoundaryState`: voltage, impedance, coupling, headroom, and latency at
  that boundary.
- `StageSignal`: the typed signal passed between stages or cells.
- `StageCoupling`: the coupling mode at a stage boundary.

The older `ComponentBoundary`, `ComponentBoundaryState`, `ComponentSignal`, and
`ComponentCoupling` names are compatibility aliases only. New code should not
use them.

## What This Means For Greybound

A Nox30 `first_stage` is not a single component. It is a stage that represents
the behavior of a small tube circuit: triode, plate load, cathode network,
coupling assumptions, and validation evidence. If the model needs the exact
resistor or capacitor values, document them as physical parts inside the stage
or circuit-cell notes.

A `tone_stack` can be a true circuit cell when it solves the passive network
with explicit source/load assumptions. A `power_stage` may still be a graybox
stage if it exposes useful current, voltage, sag, and headroom behavior without
solving every physical part.

This keeps the project honest:

- circuit descriptors can explain the model without pretending to be PCB
  artwork,
- lab reports can say whether they validate a physical part, a cell, a stage,
  or a complete device,
- future open-source readers can tell which parts are measured, inferred,
  graybox, or algorithmic.

## Acceptance Language

Use precise acceptance statements:

- "This stage is circuit-informed and validated against a NAM render."
- "This cell has a SPICE fixture and operating-point checks."
- "This physical part value is sourced with medium confidence."
- "This device is a graybox approximation, not a component-exact clone."

Avoid broad claims such as "component-accurate" unless every relevant physical
part, value, topology variant, operating point, and validation fixture is
documented.
